Pomas · Poultry Management Desktop

Pomas is a Windows desktop app for managing poultry farm operations, including batches, daily records, feed and inventory, health, egg production, sales, expenses, and reports.

Problem: helping poultry farmers organize bookkeeping and calculate business performance and profit.

My contribution

I defined the product specifications and requirements. The hardest implementation challenge I identified was packaging the Electron desktop app, which highlighted the importance of testing a distributable build, not just a development version.
