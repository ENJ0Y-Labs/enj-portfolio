How to place an order or book a service

  1. 1. Send your request: tell us what service or product you need.
  2. 2. Confirm the details: for services, we agree on the work, what you will receive, the deadline, and the price. For products, we confirm stock and price before you order.
  3. 3. Approve and pay: pay 50% of the agreed price upfront. Work can then begin.
  4. 4. Delivery: work begins after you approve the details and make the payment.
