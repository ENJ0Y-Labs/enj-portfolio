About

About Me

ENJ is a backend developer and software builder based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. I began learning software development through technical training and hands-on practice, and I apply what I learn to practical projects.

I am drawn to backend development because I enjoy the logic behind applications: how data is stored, systems communicate, and business rules work behind the interface.

Alongside software, I create graphic designs and professionally formatted Microsoft Office documents. I also organize beginner-friendly HTML and CSS materials to support my own classes and help learners practise the fundamentals.

Featured ProjectLive · In use

Pomas · Poultry Management Desktop

Pomas is a Windows desktop app for managing poultry farm operations, including batches, daily records, feed and inventory, health, egg production, sales, expenses, and reports.

Problem: helping poultry farmers organize bookkeeping and calculate business performance and profit.

My contribution

I defined the product specifications and requirements. The hardest implementation challenge I identified was packaging the Electron desktop app, which highlighted the importance of testing a distributable build, not just a development version.
