How an enquiry works

  1. 1. Send your request: describe the service or product you need.
  2. 2. Confirm the details: for services, we agree on the scope, deliverables, timeline, and quote. For products, stock and price are confirmed before you order.
  3. 3. Approve and pay: pay 50% of the agreed quote upfront to confirm the job and allow work to begin.
  4. 4. Delivery: work begins after approval and payment, according to the agreed scope.

Graphic design and Microsoft Office formatting are currently available by quotation. Web development is not yet open for booking.
